These are the limits as Pando runs today. Many come from the Cloudflare products Pando runs on; each says why.

## Pushes and files

- **One push: at most 100 MB.** That is Cloudflare's limit on one request. Split a bigger push into several.
- **One file in a repo: at most 32 MB.** That is Artifacts' limit for one file.
- `pando` checks both before git sends anything, and says which limit a push is over. `git push --no-verify` skips the check, and the server then refuses the push.
- **About 2 pushes at once per repo.** Artifacts takes about 2 writes at once; Pando queues the rest, so a repo takes about 5 to 10 small pushes a second. A queued push gives up after 120 seconds and git says the repo is busy: push again. Pando retries a refused push of up to 1 MiB once by itself.

## Branches, diffs and merges

- **The branch diff skips text files over 1 MiB** on either side.
- **A branch's commit list shows its newest 250 commits.**
- **Finding where a branch left main stops after 5,000 commits.** Past that, the answer is `too_large`.
- **After main moves,** Pando re-checks the open branches pushed in the last 7 days, at most 100, most recently pushed first. Any other branch shows "pending", with its last known answer, for at least 30 seconds; a branch list then works out at most 25 such branches at a time.

## Worktrees and shared files

- **A machine's read-only main can trail the server by up to 10 seconds** while merges keep landing in that repo. It moves at the first merge, then again once merges pause for 3 seconds, or every 10 seconds if they never pause.
- **A read-only main in a repo where the machine has no branch worktree** is not followed live. It catches up within 5 minutes, or at once when a branch worktree is added there.
- **One shared file: at most 100 MB.** A larger file stays on its own machine.
- **Every event is kept.** A repository's event log has no cap. Each event takes 0.3 to 1.3 KB, under Cloudflare's 10 GB per Durable Object: about 8 to 30 million events per repository.

## Sign-in and access

- **A browser session ends after 30 days without use.** Each use pushes the end back.
- **A revoked credential, or a removed member, keeps working for up to 15 seconds.** Each Cloudflare server remembers a sign-in check that long.
- **For up to about 15 seconds after a repository is deleted,** a git request can still be answered from a Cloudflare server that cached the repository's record. After that, git gets "this monorepo was deleted" (the server's own words).

## Your home

- **Your activity feed keeps 7 days,** up to 1,000,000 lines. Older pages are read from each repository's own log.
- **A page of activity is 1 to 200 lines** (50 by default), and reads at most 20 repositories' logs. The rest are listed as skipped.
- **A repository with more than 100 members** is read from its own log on each page, rather than copied into each member's feed.
- **A new member sees the latest 500 lines** at once; older ones come from the log.
- **After a lost connection, the home replays up to 500 lines.** Past that, the page reloads.
- **Counts can be behind:** a repository's line and the Needs you counts by up to 10 seconds (60 seconds over 100 members); a membership change reaches the live feed within 60 seconds.
- **Needs you covers 50 repositories** and 500 rows at a time.
- **At most 32,768 people can have one repository open live at once.** That is Cloudflare's limit on connections to one Durable Object.
- **One machine reports at most 1,000 worktrees.**
- **A repo's first push to main counts up to 250 commits** in the activity.

## Profiles

- **The graph shows the last 12 months**, from the Sunday 52 weeks back to today.
- **A bio is at most 160 characters;** a name and a location 100; at most 4 links, each an https address of at most 2,048 characters.
- **Days follow the viewer's time zone at today's offset:** up to an hour off across a daylight-saving change, and half-hour zones are rounded to the hour.
- **A repository's history and a merge are counted about 10 seconds per 1,000 commits,** in the background. A commit dated in the future counts on the day it landed.

## Hooks

- **5 tries per delivery,** after 10 seconds, 1 minute, 5 minutes and 30 minutes: about 36 minutes in all. Then the delivery is dead until an owner redelivers it or replays the hook.
- **Each try waits at most 10 seconds** for an answer.
- **The latest 1,000 deliveries per hook are kept,** with the last 10 tries of each. One replay re-sends at most 1,000 deliveries.
- **A payload is under about 30 KB.** Its read token lasts 30 minutes.
- **Deliveries arrive at least once and not in order.** Drop a repeat by its `seq`, and sort by it.
- **A hook never reaches a Worker on the Pando server's own Cloudflare account** through that Worker's workers.dev address: Cloudflare refuses it. The delivery shows as 404. Hooks to any other address are not affected.

## CI with Buildkite

Pando's side:

- **One Buildkite API token per Pando server.** Pando starts builds and reads their details with the server's own token, so builds start only for Buildkite organizations that token can reach. A token per repository is not built yet.
- **One Buildkite organization per repository.**
- **At most 40 calls to Buildkite a minute per repository.** Buildkite allows 50 per token; repositories sharing one token can together reach Buildkite's limit, and Pando then waits for it to reset.
- **A job's log shows its last 1 MiB.** A log over 64 MiB opens only in Buildkite.
- **The first 100 annotations and artifacts** of a build. Download links last about 60 seconds (Buildkite's).
- **30 builds a page,** 100 at most.
- **A pipeline's settings are read again at most every 5 minutes,** and a running build's details every 10 seconds.
- **Webhook bodies up to 5 MiB;** a signature is good for 5 minutes.
- **A build's clone token reads one repo for 24 to 25 hours,** which lets Buildkite's Retry work for a day after the push. Anyone who can see the pipeline in Buildkite can see it on a job's Environment tab.
- **A status token posts checks on its one commit for 24 hours.** Other hook deliveries' read tokens last 30 minutes.
- **Only branch pushes start builds.** A merge into main does not, so main's merge commits carry no check of their own.

Buildkite's free plan, after its 30-day trial (Buildkite's numbers, read 2026-10-03):

- 2,000 Linux vCPU minutes a month, on Small (2 vCPU) machines only: about 1,000 minutes of jobs.
- 20 vCPU at once: 10 jobs; the rest wait.
- A job stops after 4 hours.
- Builds and logs are kept 30 days.
- 5 users and 1 cluster.
- 15 webhooks per organization, so one Buildkite organization serves at most 15 Pando repositories.
